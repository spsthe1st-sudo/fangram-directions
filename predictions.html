The reputation ladder

"Top 2% of T20 predictors." "9 blockbusters called." Reputation is the asset — public, rankable, and attached to the Wall of OGs we already built.

For brands, natively

A brand buys the question — "Does Valmik take 3 tonight?", presented by them — plus sponsored moments and licensed brand video. It's access to an authentic fan community at its highest-emotion second. Cricket now, every vertical next. A brand pays for the question, never for the answer — clean revenue, zero talent effort.

Sequencing

Stage 1 ships Sept 1: talent commerce. This screen is Stage 2 — the daily habit that feeds it. Nothing here delays launch.

Play for points, never money

No entry fees, no cash out, non-cash rewards only. PROGA took real-money prediction apps off the board in May — the engagement space is empty and legal. We stay on the right side of that line, always.

Zero talent effort

The live calendar writes the questions — cricket now, every stage next. Talent never has to post, record, or reply for this layer to work — it runs on the moment itself, which was the original low-touch promise.

Every answer is a signal

Predictions tell us what a fan cares about at the moment they care most. That powers the feed, the shop, and eventually the brand marketplace — intent data Instagram doesn't have.
