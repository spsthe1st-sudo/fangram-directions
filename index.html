FanGramDesign directions · v2

Twelve ways FanGram could look and feel.

FanGram is a talent-monetization platform with three sides: the talent people follow, the fans who show up, and the brands who want in. Every direction below is the full experience — the talent page, all five offerings, an order moment and a delivery moment — rebuilt brick by brick in a distinct design language.
